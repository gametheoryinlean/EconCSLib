# EFG mathematical provenance

This matrix distinguishes textbook mathematics from EconCSLib
representations and bounded interfaces. A citation supports only the row's
stated hypotheses and conclusion. Exact preservation, compilation, and
restart theorems are generally library-specific even when the objects they
transport are standard.

## Release scope

This release contains the completed results in the table. Sequential-equilibrium
and arbitrary-measure pure-strategy prototypes and their proof plans are
excluded. Bounded finite-rational Kuhn realization and well-founded determinacy
with explicit finite action data retain exactly their proved hypotheses;
neither is an unrestricted infinite-game theorem.
